# Global Digital Banking Research

## Purpose

This repository serves as a comprehensive mapping of digital banking services, features, and products from players around the world. The goal is to identify successful banking products and features across different markets to inform product strategy for Ton's expansion into retail banking in Brazil.

## Research Objectives

1. **Identify global banking players** by country and category
2. **Map features and services** across different product categories:
   - Credit card products
   - Lending services
   - Insurance products
   - Travel services
   - Rewards programs
   - Marketplaces
   - Investment services
   - Financial organization tools
   - Cybersecurity and fraud prevention
   - Payment services
   - Savings and deposits
   - And more...

3. **Analyze successful implementations** to inform Ton's product roadmap

## Analysis Approach

### For Human Researchers
1. Read [ANALYSIS_METHODOLOGY.md](ANALYSIS_METHODOLOGY.md)
2. Choose a banking player from `/countries/` lists
3. Follow the research process to create a player profile
4. Document findings in `/players/[country-code]-[player-name].md`
5. Cross-reference features in `/categories/` files
