# United States - Major Retail Banking Players

## Traditional Banks (with Digital Services)

### 1. **Chase (JPMorgan Chase)**
- Customer Base: ~60 million digital customers
- Key Digital Products: Chase Mobile app, Chase.com
- Notable: Largest bank in the US by assets

### 2. **Bank of America**
- Customer Base: ~42 million digital users
- Key Digital Products: BofA Mobile Banking, Erica (AI assistant)
- Notable: Second largest bank in the US

### 3. **Wells Fargo**
- Customer Base: ~33 million digital customers
- Key Digital Products: Wells Fargo Mobile
- Notable: Third largest bank in the US

### 4. **Citibank**
- Customer Base: ~16 million US digital customers
- Key Digital Products: Citi Mobile
- Notable: Global presence, strong credit card portfolio

### 5. **Capital One**
- Customer Base: ~50 million accounts
- Key Digital Products: Capital One Mobile, digital-first approach
- Notable: Known for innovation and tech-forward banking

### 6. **US Bank**
- Customer Base: ~13 million digital users
- Key Digital Products: US Bank Mobile App
- Notable: Fifth largest commercial bank in the US

### 7. **PNC Bank**
- Customer Base: ~10 million digital customers
- Key Digital Products: PNC Mobile, Virtual Wallet
- Notable: Strong presence in mid-Atlantic and Midwest

### 8. **Truist Bank**
- Customer Base: ~7 million digital users
- Key Digital Products: Truist Mobile
- Notable: Merger of BB&T and SunTrust (2019)

### 9. **TD Bank**
- Customer Base: ~9 million US customers
- Key Digital Products: TD Bank Mobile
- Notable: Canadian-owned, strong East Coast presence

### 10. **Fifth Third Bank**
- Customer Base: ~2.5 million digital users
- Key Digital Products: Fifth Third Mobile
- Notable: Midwest and Southeast presence

## Digital-First Banks / Neobanks

### 1. **Chime**
- Customer Base: ~15 million accounts (2023)
- Founded: 2013
- Key Features: No-fee banking, early direct deposit, automatic savings
- Notable: Largest US neobank

### 2. **Current**
- Customer Base: ~4 million members
- Founded: 2015
- Key Features: Teen banking, faster direct deposits, points rewards
- Notable: Focus on younger demographics

### 3. **Varo Bank**
- Customer Base: ~2 million accounts
- Founded: 2015
- Key Features: First US neobank with national bank charter (2020)
- Notable: No minimum balance, no monthly fees

### 4. **SoFi (Social Finance)**
- Customer Base: ~6 million members
- Founded: 2011
- Key Features: Started as student loan refi, now full banking + investing
- Notable: Acquired bank charter (2022), Super App strategy

### 5. **Ally Bank**
- Customer Base: ~2.5 million deposit customers
- Founded: 2009 (from GMAC)
- Key Features: Online-only, high-yield savings, auto financing
- Notable: One of the largest online-only banks

### 6. **Marcus by Goldman Sachs**
- Customer Base: ~8 million customers
- Founded: 2016
- Key Features: High-yield savings, personal loans, no-fee banking
- Notable: Goldman Sachs' consumer banking arm

### 7. **Discover Bank**
- Customer Base: ~60 million cardholders
- Founded: 1985 (credit cards), banking since 2007
- Key Features: Cashback debit, online banking, no fees
- Notable: Part of Discover Financial Services

### 8. **Revolut**
- Customer Base: ~500K US customers (30M+ globally)
- Founded: 2015 (UK), US launch 2020
- Key Features: Multi-currency, crypto, stock trading
- Notable: Global player expanding in US

### 9. **N26**
- Customer Base: Limited US presence (focused on Europe)
- Founded: 2013 (Germany), US operations suspended 2022
- Key Features: European-style mobile banking
- Notable: Withdrew from US market in 2022

### 10. **Dave**
- Customer Base: ~10 million users
- Founded: 2017
- Key Features: Cash advances, budgeting tools, no-fee banking
- Notable: Focus on avoiding overdraft fees

## Payment Apps with Banking Services

### 1. **PayPal**
- Customer Base: ~400 million active accounts globally (~200M in US)
- Key Features: Savings accounts (through Synchrony Bank), debit card
- Notable: Acquired Venmo

### 2. **Venmo**
- Customer Base: ~90 million users
- Key Features: P2P payments, Venmo debit card, direct deposit
- Notable: Owned by PayPal, social payment feed

### 3. **Cash App (Block, formerly Square)**
- Customer Base: ~50 million active users
- Founded: 2013
- Key Features: Cash Card (debit), Bitcoin, stocks, tax filing
- Notable: Strong in P2P and Bitcoin transactions

### 4. **Zelle**
- Customer Base: ~120 million enrolled users
- Founded: 2017
- Key Features: Bank-to-bank transfers, embedded in bank apps
- Notable: Owned by Early Warning Services (consortium of major banks)

## Notes

This list focuses on players with significant retail/consumer banking presence in the US market. The landscape is highly competitive with:
- Traditional banks investing heavily in digital transformation
- Neobanks challenging with no-fee, mobile-first experiences
- Payment apps adding banking features
- Investment platforms adding banking services
- Specialty players targeting specific demographics (teens, families, etc.)

**Market Trends:**
- Consolidation among neobanks
- Traditional banks acquiring fintech capabilities
- Focus on embedded finance
- AI/ML for personalization
- Open banking APIs
- Crypto integration
