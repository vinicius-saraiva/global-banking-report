# Chime - United States (Example Analysis)

> **Note**: This is an example/template showing the analysis format. A complete analysis would have more detail.

## Overview
- **Type**: Neobank (Partner bank model)
- **Founded**: 2013
- **Headquarters**: San Francisco, California
- **Regulatory Status**: Partner banks (The Bancorp Bank, Stride Bank)
- **Customer Base**: ~15 million accounts (2023)
- **Markets**: United States only
- **Website**: https://www.chime.com
- **App Store**: iOS & Android

## Business Model
- **Revenue Sources**:
  - Interchange fees from debit card transactions
  - Overdraft fees on optional SpotMe feature (tips-based)
  - Optional expedited card shipping
- **Pricing Tiers**: Single free tier (no monthly fees)
- **Target Audience**:
  - Millennials and Gen Z
  - Underbanked consumers
  - Fee-sensitive customers
  - Early direct deposit seekers

## Product Catalog

### 1. Account Types & Core Banking

#### Chime Checking Account
- **Category**: Account Types > Checking
- **Description**: Fee-free mobile checking account
- **Availability**: All users
- **Pricing**: Free (no monthly fees, no minimum balance)
- **Limits**: No limit on deposits; daily spending limits apply to debit card
- **Unique Characteristics**:
  - No overdraft fees
  - No monthly maintenance fees
  - No minimum balance requirements
- **Status**: Active

#### Chime Savings Account
- **Category**: Account Types > Savings
- **Description**: High-yield savings with automatic savings features
- **Availability**: All users
- **Pricing**: Free; competitive APY (varies)
- **Limits**: 6 withdrawals per month (federal regulation)
- **Unique Characteristics**:
  - Automatic round-up savings
  - Save When You Get Paid (auto-percentage)
  - Save When You Spend (merchant-based savings)
- **Status**: Active

### 2. Cards

#### Chime Visa Debit Card
- **Category**: Cards > Debit Cards (Physical)
- **Description**: Visa debit card with no fees
- **Availability**: All account holders
- **Pricing**: Free
- **Limits**: Daily ATM withdrawal limits ($515), daily purchase limit ($2,500)
- **Unique Characteristics**:
  - Fee-free at 60,000+ ATMs (MoneyPass, Visa Plus Alliance)
  - No foreign transaction fees
  - Instant transaction notifications
- **Status**: Active

#### Virtual Debit Card
- **Category**: Cards > Debit Cards (Virtual)
- **Description**: Instant virtual card available before physical card arrives
- **Availability**: All new users
- **Pricing**: Free
- **Unique Characteristics**:
  - Available immediately upon account opening
  - Can be added to Apple Pay, Google Pay instantly
- **Status**: Active

#### Chime Credit Builder Secured Visa
- **Category**: Cards > Credit Cards (Secured)
- **Description**: Secured credit card for building credit with no annual fees
- **Availability**: Chime account holders (opt-in)
- **Pricing**: Free (no annual fee, no interest charges)
- **Unique Characteristics**:
  - Works like a debit card (charged immediately from Credit Builder account)
  - Reports to all 3 major credit bureaus
  - No credit check to apply
  - No interest charges (not a traditional line of credit)
- **Launch Date**: 2020
- **Status**: Active

### 3. Payments & Transfers

#### Early Direct Deposit
- **Category**: Payments > Direct Deposit
- **Description**: Get paid up to 2 days early with direct deposit
- **Availability**: All users with direct deposit setup
- **Pricing**: Free
- **Unique Characteristics**:
  - Paycheck available as soon as Chime receives notice (often 2 days early)
  - Works with government benefits too
  - No fees or strings attached
- **Status**: Active

#### P2P Transfers (Pay Friends)
- **Category**: Payments > P2P Payments
- **Description**: Send money to other Chime members instantly
- **Availability**: All users
- **Pricing**: Free
- **Limits**: Up to $2,000 per month
- **Status**: Active

#### External Bank Transfers
- **Category**: Payments > Bank Transfers
- **Description**: Transfer money to/from external bank accounts
- **Availability**: All users
- **Pricing**: Free
- **Limits**: Varies by account history
- **Status**: Active

### 4. Credit & Lending

#### SpotMe Overdraft Coverage
- **Category**: Credit & Lending > Overdraft Protection
- **Description**: Fee-free overdraft coverage up to $200
- **Availability**: Eligible users (requires $200+ monthly direct deposit)
- **Pricing**: Free (optional tips accepted)
- **Limits**: $20 initially, up to $200 with account history
- **Unique Characteristics**:
  - No overdraft fees
  - No interest charged
  - Automatic coverage on debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals
  - Tips are optional, not required
- **User Requirements**:
  - $200+ in monthly direct deposits
  - Account in good standing for 30+ days
- **Launch Date**: 2019
- **Status**: Active

## Competitive Advantages

1. **Fee-Free Model**: No monthly fees, no overdraft fees, no minimum balance
2. **Early Direct Deposit**: Industry-leading 2-day early access
3. **SpotMe**: Unique fee-free overdraft alternative
4. **Scale**: Largest US neobank by users
5. **Simple UX**: Easy-to-use mobile-first interface
6. **Automatic Savings**: Multiple automated savings features

**Opportunities**:
- Investment products
